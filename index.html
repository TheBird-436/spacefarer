SPACEFARER · SOLAR SYSTEM EXPLORER

About Spacefarer

Spacefarer is an interactive guide to the eight planets of our solar system. Explore each world, discover its facts and orbital data, and learn as you go.

Explore the planets

Select a planet to see its profile, image, key measurements, and a featured fact. Open Deep Data for more details, or explore the moons that orbit each world.

Learn as you go

Test what you know with planet quizzes, earn experience as you discover new worlds, and collect badges along the way.

See the bigger picture

Use the orbit map and scale comparison to explore how the planets fit into our solar system, or take a break with the Space Dodge mini-game.
